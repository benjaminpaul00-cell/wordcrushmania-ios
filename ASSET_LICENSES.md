# Asset provenance

The fantasy forest, logo cutout, raccoon avatar and four-booster atlas were copied from the owner's private WordCrushMania Android repository. That repository describes them as generated/original project artwork; the publisher remains responsible for the rights to any supplied visual reference. The app icon is a resized opaque packaging of the existing logo over a purple background. Booster sprites are selected from the atlas at runtime.

The English themed vocabulary was extracted from the existing Dictionary, retaining its allowed-word filtering. The source project's ASSET_LICENSES.md dedicates that curated vocabulary and its generated WAV files to CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . The six bundled files are music.wav, select.wav, word.wav, combo.wav, failure.wav and victory.wav.

The externally supplied MP3 playlist and OGG sound effects are not bundled. Their source redistribution terms need separate review before inclusion.

System symbols are rendered through SF Symbols on Apple platforms. Google Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform, Swift and other dependencies retain their respective licenses. No blanket open-source license is granted for the private application source by this file.
